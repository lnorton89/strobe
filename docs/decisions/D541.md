# D541: the crate graph, `unsafe`, feature configurations, rustdoc, workflows and scripts are checked, and the build names its profile (2026-11-03, engineering audit)

**Context.** The audit's "further improvements" table listed checks the
workspace did not yet make. All of them were small, and each was the
compiler's or a linter's job, not a person's:

- Nothing stopped a `Cargo.toml` line from putting the renderer under the
  audio crate, or the app under a library: the layers in
  `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` were prose.
- `unsafe` was kept to the GL boundary by a line scan
  (`strobe_conventions::unsafe_boundary`, D41), which a macro expansion
  can slip past.
- `--all-features` everywhere meant feature unification could hide a
  missing `cfg` in `strobe-audio` without `capture` or `strobe-shadertoy`
  without `net`. Only `strobe-io` without `midi` was checked.
- Rustdoc never ran in CI, so broken intra-doc links accumulated: about
  thirty, once it did.
- The workflows and the shell and PowerShell scripts had no linter beyond
  the SHA and permissions convention (D54).
- `PROFILE` is only `debug` or `release`, so a `release-fast` build (no
  fat LTO, D487) called itself `release` in `--diagnose` and in every
  crash report.

**Decision.**

- **`strobe_conventions::architecture`** reads `cargo metadata`'s resolved
  graph and walks each ruled crate's normal dependencies, transitively:
  - the scene model reaches no GPU, window, network or script engine;
  - audio, script, show control, playlist, remote and projector reach no
    renderer or UI toolkit;
  - the renderer reaches no importer, scorer or subsystem above it;
  - diagnostics reach none of the others;
  - nothing links the app, the bench or the tooling.

  Dev-dependencies are a separate graph and are allowed (a renderer test
  may import a converter). A failure prints the path, such as
  `strobe-audio -> helper -> strobe-gfx`.
- **`unsafe_code = "deny"`** is a workspace lint. The two allowed places
  carry `#[expect(unsafe_code, reason = …)]` on their module (`gl` in
  strobe-gfx, `native` in strobe-diag), and the expectation fails if they
  ever stop needing it. The line scan stays as the textual half: it also
  checks one operation per leaf and no `pub unsafe fn`.
- **The `check` lane on Linux** also clippies `strobe-audio` without
  `capture` and `strobe-shadertoy` without `net`, beside `strobe-io`
  without `midi`. It also builds the docs:
  `cargo doc --workspace --no-deps --all-features --document-private-items`
  with `-D warnings`. These are an application's internal docs, so private
  items are documented and `rustdoc::private_intra_doc_links` is allowed
  through `RUSTDOCFLAGS` (not an attribute). Every other rustdoc warning
  fails. `strobe-gfx` without `native` is not checked on a native host: its
  intended configuration is wasm, which backlog item 413 covers.
- **A `lint` lane**: actionlint (1.7.12) over the workflows, which also
  runs shellcheck over every `run:` block, and shellcheck (0.11.0) over the
  tracked `.sh` scripts, in the `rustfmt` job; PSScriptAnalyzer (1.25.0),
  warnings and errors, over the tracked `.ps1` scripts in the Windows job,
  under the PowerShell 5.1 that runs them.
- **The build names its profile.** strobe-diag's build script takes the
  profile from the folder cargo builds into (`target/<profile>/…`), so
  `--diagnose` and crash reports say `release-fast`, `release` or
  `release-thin` (D535).

**Trade-off.** The architecture rules are a list someone must edit when
the design really changes. That is the point: the change then has a
decision. `cargo metadata` adds a few seconds to the conventions tests.

**Test.**
- `architecture`'s tests: the real graph passes, a forbidden edge planted
  through a third crate is found with its path, a dev edge is allowed, and
  a library linking the app fails.
- The workspace passes clippy with `unsafe_code` denied.
- The three no-default configurations pass clippy on LIGHTNING.
- Rustdoc passes with the flags above after the link fixes in this change.
- `xtask`'s planner tests cover the new steps.

**Would reopen:** a crate that genuinely needs a second unsafe boundary (a
new `ALLOWED` entry and its own `expect`), or the wasm build joining CI,
which would add `strobe-gfx` without `native` for `wasm32`.
