# D550: background compiles leave the output four fifths of the time, and a rebuilt context reloads the thumbnails (2026-11-04, mini PC pre-release pass)

**Context.** The mini PC (AMD Vega 3, the venue machine) ran the v0.3.0
pre-release checks on 6612575 and found two faults on the show's path.

- **The launch prerender starved the output.** D542 spaced its steps 50 ms
  apart, which suited a desktop compiling a scene in tens of
  milliseconds. On the Vega 3 a compile took hundreds of milliseconds,
  one 4.3 s. The minute it took to prerender 116 scenes logged 7.4 fps,
  34% missed refreshes, a 4,315 ms worst gap and three audio buffer
  glitches. The D547 thumbnail job loads scenes back to back the same
  way.
- **A rebuilt GL context left the thumbnails behind.** The rebuild (D66)
  makes a new control window, egui context and painter, but the
  thumbnail cache kept textures uploaded through the old one. Each frame
  drew nothing where they were and logged a "Failed to find texture"
  warning for each: 103,675 lines in 142 s after `lose-context@60`. A
  four-hour show would write millions.

**Decision.**

- **A background step waits four times its own cost.**
  `prerender::next_step` puts the next step at the later of 50 ms (D542's
  floor) and four times what the last step held the frame loop for. So
  the output keeps at least four fifths of the time on any machine. The
  launch prerender and the thumbnail job's scene loads both use it; the
  thumbnail job's frames at 320x180 are not spaced, since they are cheap.
  On a fast machine nothing changes: a 2-12 ms step still waits 50 ms. On
  the Vega 3 the library takes about four times longer to prepare, and
  the output draws through it.
- **The thumbnail cache drops its textures when the context is rebuilt**
  (`Thumbs::forget_textures`). Each picture is decoded again off the UI
  thread the next time its row is drawn. One still decoding uploads into
  the new context, and a broken one stays marked missing. The desktop
  viewer already re-uploaded its texture on a new context, and nothing
  else keeps an egui texture across frames.
- **A smaller fault from the same pass:** with `--scene` or
  `--screenshot`, launch no longer logs that the last session's scene "is
  not in the library". The command line chose the scene; nothing was
  missing.

**Trade-off.** A slow machine's library is prepared more slowly. It is
still prepared, and a scene played before its turn loads fresh as it did
before D542. One long compile still holds the frame loop for its whole
length, as a load by hand does, until `GL_KHR_parallel_shader_compile`
can move compiles off it (D542's reopen).

**Test.**
- `prerender` test
  `the_output_keeps_four_fifths_of_the_time_however_slow_a_step_is`:
  costs from 0 to 4.3 s, with the floor kept.
- `ui::thumbs` test
  `a_rebuilt_context_drops_uploaded_pictures_and_keeps_the_rest`.
- On a real GPU this is still to show: a launch prerender on the mini PC
  with no minute under 50 fps, and `lose-context@60` with thumbnails
  back and no egui warnings. The results go in `hardware/MINIPC.md`.

**Would reopen:** a machine where four times the cost still shows, or
parallel shader compile becoming available on the drivers that matter.
