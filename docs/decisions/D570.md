# D570: NDI and Spout output leave through one seam, NDI over a readback and Spout over the texture (2026-11-26)

**Context.** Backlog M-06 was "deferred research; not a v1 blocker", and it
asked for "SDK/runtime licensing, Windows packaging, pixel format, frame
pacing, GPU readback versus shared textures, latency and failure handling"
before any implementation. None of that had been done: PLAN.md held a
`grafton-ndi` note and a PBO-ring sentence, TORNADO T21 settled that these
are outputs and not `syn_MediaType` inputs, D248 was waiting for an output
that carries alpha, and D181/Q20 left blackout's effect on a shared output
open. The research is
[`research/m06-ndi-spout.md`](../research/m06-ndi-spout.md); this entry
records the choices it makes.

**Decision.**

- **One crate, `strobe-output`**, owns the seam between the engine and a
  destination: the `Frame` (RGBA8, top row first, in a reused buffer), the
  packing a bytes-based sender wants (BGRA, and UYVY 4:2:2 in BT.709
  limited range at a caller's stride), a `Sink` trait for one destination,
  `Output` around it with the show's failure policy, and probes for the
  optional runtimes. It depends on no Strobe crate and never on GL, like
  `strobe-capture`. The renderer hands it pixels, or lends it a texture.
- **No SDK at build time for either.** `grafton-ndi` 3.0 loads an NDI 6
  runtime at runtime (beside the executable, `NDI_RUNTIME_DIR_V6`, then the
  loader's own paths), and Spout's library is found by the loader. So both
  features compile on every target, `cargo clippy --all-features` and the
  Windows cross-check keep working, and a machine without either still
  builds the app. Neither is on by default.
- **The tap is after the output post stage**, on the picture and not the
  letterboxed window: the post stage's target when it ran, else the
  compositor's, else the scene's output — the same choice a hidden window's
  screenshot already makes. Post is after the compositor, so a crossfade
  goes out as one picture, and `master`, colour, mirrors and `Limit Colors`
  reach the stream as they reach the projector.
- **Blackout does not gate the stream.** It is a present-time clear of the
  shown window (D135, D181); a shared output follows `master`, so the panic
  key darkens the room without cutting a recording, and a cue or a macro can
  send black when the stream should go too. Q20's other half (should blackout
  pause the clock) is unchanged: no.
- **`Alpha Channel` unblocks (D248)**: the frame and BGRA carry alpha
  unchanged, and the meta control lands in the post stage, which is where
  the alpha is composed, not in a sender. Until an output is on nothing
  produces a non-opaque picture, so the control stays out of the bank until
  the tap exists.
- **NDI sends UYVY first and BGRA as the fallback**, at the output's size,
  with `clock_video(false)` so the show's cadence is the only clock, and
  through the SDK's asynchronous submission with two reclaimed frames rather
  than a second copy. 1080p60 is 249 MB/s in UYVY and 498 in BGRA; the
  picture's cost on the frame is the readback, which is why it runs through
  a full-size pixel-pack ring with fences, as `textureFinalPass` does at
  64x36.
- **Spout is the zero-copy Windows path**, sharing the post stage's texture
  on the render thread with the GL context current. NDI is the
  interoperable path; Spout is the free one.
- **One `Output` is one named destination**, so several senders at once
  are several outputs, each on its own stable name (issue #116 asks for
  exactly that, and for the names to be the operator's). Two senders with
  one name are refused rather than left to fight over the receiver.
- **Spout input is not this decision.** A receiver is a media source, the
  mirror of the webcam (D528), and belongs with M-04's family: `spout:<name>`
  as a media choice, `syn_MediaType` 3, one reused texture.
- **Failure is the show's, not the sender's**: said once per outage through
  the failure path with an action, the sink closed and dropped, the frame
  dropped and counted, reopened by the host after a backoff, and the runtime
  probed *before* a sender is made so the console names what to install.
- **NDI's licence duties are part of the feature**: a link to
  https://ndi.video beside every place NDI is chosen, in the docs and on the
  website, and no redistribution of the NDI tools. A YouTube-style "this
  software uses NDI" line in Settings > Output, USER_GUIDE and
  COMPATIBILITY is enough.

**Not decided here** (they need the owner, and are listed in the research
page): whether Spout goes through `spout-rs` or `SpoutLibrary.dll` and one
new unsafe leaf; whether the standard NDI runtime has a session limit;
whether the send rate gets a lever; and whether audio goes over NDI.

**Test.** `strobe-output` is tested on every OS with no runtime: the packing
against the BT.709 equations for the primaries, black and white, an odd
width and a padded stride; the failure policy against a fake sink (one close
per outage, counters, reopening only when down, a stopped output that is not
a failure); the probes against a scratch folder. **Not verified:** anything
that needs an NDI runtime or a Spout receiver, and the frame cost, which is
M-06's acceptance test on the T480.

**Would reopen:** the readback costing more than about 1 ms at 1080p on an
HD 4000 (then a smaller capture size, or UYVY in the pack buffer via
`glReadPixels`' own format); `spout-rs` remaining unpinnable (then the
`SpoutLibrary.dll` leaf and its unsafe-boundary entry); NDI's terms turning
out to forbid a bundled runtime or require more than a link; or a venue
network that cannot carry 250 MB/s, which would put the send rate and a
compressed path back on the table.
