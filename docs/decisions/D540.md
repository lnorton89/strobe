# D540: thumbnails are read by their bytes and fill the card (2026-11-03, owner's request)

Synesthesia's scenes showed in the library either letterboxed (a square
picture centred in a 16:9 card, with dark bars) or as a generated letter
where their picture was plainly there.

- **Read by what the file is.** Many of the thumbnails Synesthesia ships
  are JPEGs named `.png` (`SkaiWalkerOG`'s `thumb.png`, `deeper_v2.png`,
  `audio_indicators.png`). `image::open` picks the decoder from the
  extension, so they failed and drew the letter. `ui::thumbs::decode` now
  guesses the format from the file's first bytes
  (`ImageReader::with_guessed_format`), so a picture of any supported
  format decodes whatever it is called.
- **Fill, not fit.** A thumbnail is scaled to fill `MAX_SIZE` (160x90) on
  the worker, cropping the middle, and the card draws it to fill its
  picture area too (`browser::cover_uv`), under the card's rounded top
  corners. A square picture loses a strip at the top and bottom instead of
  sitting between bars. Filling at decode keeps the pixels the card shows
  at full resolution rather than upscaling a 90x90 square.

**Evidence:** `ui::thumbs` test
`a_jpeg_named_png_and_a_square_picture_both_fill_the_card`, `ui::browser`
test `a_picture_of_another_shape_is_cropped_in_the_middle_to_fill`, and a
`--screenshot` of the library on a machine with Synesthesia installed: all
88 of its scenes show their picture edge to edge.

**Would reopen:** a scene whose picture matters at its edges (text, a
logo), which would want fit for that scene, or a sharper thumbnail on a
high-DPI screen, which would raise `MAX_SIZE` against its texture memory.
