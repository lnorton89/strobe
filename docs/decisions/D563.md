# D563: a short dropdown shows its options side by side, and a mapped control says so on its row (2026-11-07, backlog U-11, on the owner's go-ahead to keep working)

**Context.** The value-bar mockup the owner chose (D510) also showed a
segmented control for a dropdown of a few options and a mark on a row for
its MIDI or OSC mapping. Neither was built: a dropdown was a combo box
whatever its size, so seeing its options took a click, and nothing on a row
said a controller drove it, so a person turning a knob on the screen could
not tell that a fader would move it back.

**Decision.**

- **A dropdown of up to five options shows them side by side** at the row's
  right end, where the combo box was, the chosen one filled in the accent.
  Each option is its own target: a click, or Space or Enter with it focused,
  chooses it, and screen readers hear radio buttons. The cells are equal when
  the widest label fits that way and each its own width when only that fits
  (`segment_widths`). The row keeps 72 points for its name, so with more than
  five options, or labels too long for the row, the combo box stays. A one-
  letter option is still 28 points wide.
- **A mapped control's row shows the mapping's source**: `CC 21`, `Note 36`,
  `Bend`, `PC 5` or the OSC address (`Source::tag`), in an outlined mark left
  of the number or the widget. It is outlined where `MACRO` is filled, so the
  two read apart. A second mapping makes it `CC 21 +1`, and the tooltip lists
  every mapping in full, with its channel and device. An address longer than
  96 points is cut short with an ellipsis, and a row too narrow to keep its
  name drops the mark first.
- **What counts as mapped** is `Engine::sources_by_control`: every switched-on
  mapping whose target finds the control the way the mapper finds it,
  aliases and a macro's own target included. A mapping that cannot drive the
  control (switched off, an XY pad or a colour named without its component,
  a target in the other bank) makes no mark. The marks are recomputed each
  frame the inspector draws, from the mappings the engine holds, so they stay
  current with learn and with edits in Mappings.
- **The macro cards are unchanged.** They keep the slider rail (D510, D511)
  and the combo box until the owner decides whether they take the value bar
  and its gestures (U-11).

**Test.** `controls`' tests: which option sets fit side by side and which keep
the combo box; the mark's text for none, one and three mappings; a three-option
dropdown draws its labels and a click on its last option chooses it (it fails
with side-by-side options switched off); a six-option dropdown keeps the combo
box; a mapped slider draws its mark and an unmapped one does not. `engine`'s
test: listed once per source, through a macro, not when switched off or
without a component, and kept to its bank. Captures in screenshot mode of
`03-kaleido-tunnel` (a four-option dropdown, an XY pad on two OSC addresses, a
bang on a note, a speed slider on a CC, and `master` on a CC) on the Controls
page and in the side panel, at both densities, on LIGHTNING. The first
Compact capture showed the chosen option's fill shorter than its digit in the
side panel. The options were given the row's height less 2 points.

**Would reopen:** a person reading the mark as something to click (it is not:
mappings are edited in Mappings), or a scene whose short dropdowns read worse
side by side than in a list.
