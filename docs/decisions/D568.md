# D568: cargo-deny ignores RUSTSEC-2026-0543, `chrono` unmaintained, until its two calls move (2026-11-26)

**Context.** RustSec now lists every `chrono` release as unmaintained
(RUSTSEC-2026-0543), and cargo-deny's advisories check fails on it. It is not
a vulnerability. Strobe depends on `chrono` 0.4.45 directly, in two places:
`strobe-app`'s `localtime` reads the platform's UTC offset for `strobe_Date`,
and `strobe-projector`'s `audit` writes a local ISO timestamp on each line.
The advisory suggests `jiff` or `time`. `time` cannot read the local offset
once a process has threads on Linux, and `jiff` would be a new dependency.
Choosing between them is a decision of its own, not a CI fix.

**Decision.** `deny.toml` ignores that one advisory, with its reason, as D530
does for `paste`. Moving the two calls to a maintained crate is backlog R-10.
The same CI run also failed on RUSTSEC-2026-0592 (`url` 2.5.8, a stack
overflow in `Url::origin()` on nested `blob:` URLs, pulled in by
`webbrowser` through `egui-winit`). That one is fixed by updating to `url`
2.5.9, not ignored.

**Would reopen:** a vulnerability reported against `chrono`, or R-10 done,
which removes the entry.
